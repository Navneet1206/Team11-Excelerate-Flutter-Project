# SkillTrack Pro — User Experience Manual

Welcome to SkillTrack Pro, your institution's central hub for learning management and professional growth. This guide outlines the platform's features and workflows under the **Digital Scholastic** interface.

---

## 🔐 Getting Started
- **Institutional Login**: Use your secure credentials to sign in. The system automatically routes you to your specialized dashboard based on your role (**Learner**, **Mentor**, or **Admin**).
- **Security Check**: For your protection, all sessions are token-based. Always sign out when using shared devices.

---

## 🎓 Learner Experience

### Your Learning Journey
Upon login, you'll see your **Learning Journey** dashboard. It features a high-level summary of your productivity:
- **Total Tasks**: Every deliverable assigned to you.
- **Approved**: High-priority tasks successfully validated by your mentor.
- **Completion Trail**: A visual percentage tracker showing your progress toward 100% curriculum completion.

### Submitting Deliverables
Open any program and expand a **Module** to view your deliverables.
1. **Initial Review**: Read the task description, check the **Deadline Threshold**, and copy any **Knowledge Bank** links.
2. **Execution**: Once your work is ready, provide a direct URL (GitHub, Drive, etc.) and add **Submission Notes**.
3. **Synchronization**: After confirming your submission, the task is locked for mentor review. You can monitor its status (Submitted, Approved, or Rejected) directly from your journey view.

### Sentiment Analysis (Feedback)
Once you reach **100% completion**, you can unlock the **Program Feedback** section. Share your qualitative thoughts and provide a star rating to help us improve the curriculum.

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Mentor Experience

### Cohort Oversight
The **Mentor Dashboard** provides a real-time pulse of your assigned learners:
- **Pending Reviews**: A prioritized count of submissions awaiting your decision.
- **Learner Ranking**: A leaderboard showing the most active performers in your cohort.
- **Learning Trails**: View any learner's vertical activity timeline to understand their development pace.

### The Grading Hub
1. Open a submission from your **Review Inbox**.
2. Tap **Edit** to initialize the grading process.
3. Assign a **Performance Score** (0-100) and provide constructive feedback.
4. Finalize the review to instantly notify the learner of their status.

---

## 🏛️ Administrator Experience

### Management Hub
The **Admin Console** is the system's brain:
- **User Provisioning**: Handle onboarding for new learners, mentors, and administrators. Use role-based filters for quick bulk management.
- **Curriculum Architecture**: Create programs and initialize high-level milestones.
- **Milestone Drafting**: Use the "Draft Challenge" system to provision new tasks with deadlines and resources.
- **Delegate Controls**: Use the Management Hub to delegate subject matter experts (Mentors) to specific programs.

### Governance & Auditing
Access the **Audit Logs** to view a chronological feed of all institutional activity. Every administrative action—from user creation to program delegation—is recorded for transparency.

---

## 🛠️ Performance & Connectivity
- **Instant Refresh**: Pull down on any dashboard to fetch the latest state from our backend.
- **Network Status**: The app will notify you via a **Digital Scholastic Info Banner** if the network threshold is exceeded or if synchronization fails.
- **System Integrity**: Remember, the backend is the single source of truth—if you see "Locked for Review", your submission has been safely recorded and cannot be altered until reviewed.
